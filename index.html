About

Where design instinct meets code.

What first drew me in was the creative side of the web — then I fell in love with building it.

I'm a Computer Science student with a strong interest in Front-End Development, Web Design, and UI/UX. I enjoy thinking about how a website should look, how users will interact with it, and how different design elements can work together.

As I started learning and practicing more, I became increasingly interested in coding itself — using HTML and CSS to turn ideas into real, responsive websites. I'm especially drawn to projects for brands, companies, and e-commerce businesses that give me room to be creative while staying clean, simple, and professional.

I pay close attention to details, because I believe the little things — spacing, color, motion — make the biggest difference.
